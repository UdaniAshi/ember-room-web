Programme 04

Guided Focus — careful coaching

For those who need more thoughtful, attentive coaching: whether you're returning from injury, a senior client looking for a measured approach, or simply someone who wants extra care built into every session.

Some clients need a coach who slows down, listens carefully, and builds from where the body actually is — not where it used to be. Guided Focus is designed for anyone who benefits from a more considered pace: recovering from injury, managing chronic pain, post-surgical rehabilitation, or a senior client who wants coaching that works with their body at this stage of life.

Your coach works closely with your timeline, beginning at what's safe, building carefully, and only progressing when you're truly ready.

In-depth initial assessment

Your coach takes time to understand your injury history, current limitations, and what movement feels safe — before any programme is written.

Gradual, structured progression

No rushing. The programme advances only when you're physically ready, with regular check-ins to adjust as you recover.

Coordination with your medical team

If you're working with a physio or specialist, your coach can work alongside their guidance — not against it.

Before you book

Common questions

Do sessions expire?
Yes, we recommend completing 10 sessions within a month to get the most out of your programme. Your coach will help you set a schedule that keeps you on track from the start.
Can I upgrade from 10 sessions to more later?
Yes. If you want to extend after starting, you can top up and the bulk savings will apply to the additional sessions. Ask your coach for the current top-up rates.
Is there a fee for the initial assessment?
A standalone assessment is 5,000 LKR. If you book a programme at the same time, the assessment is included at no extra charge; it's only billed separately if you come in for a consultation without committing to a package on the day.
What does the Ember Room app do?
The app gives you access to your programme, session logs, progress benchmarks and direct messaging with your coach. Available on iOS and Android as part of any active programme.
What is a Discovery Session?
A paid introductory session (5,000 LKR) where you meet your coach, go through your initial assessment and see the space. If you go ahead and book a programme on the same day, the fee is waived; you pay only for the programme.
I have an injury — which programme is right for me?
The Guided Focus programme is built for exactly this: clients who need more careful, attentive coaching. Get in touch and we'll discuss your situation before recommending anything.
